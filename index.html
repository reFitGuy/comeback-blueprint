Progression Rules

How an exercise clears its blueprint

  1. Ready: both working sets reach the current ceiling at effort 3 or easier for two sessions in a row.
  2. Still hard: ceiling reps at effort 4–5 do not count. Hold the variation until the work feels Medium or easier.
  3. Fast track: if the very first logged session already clears the ceiling at effort 3 or easier, move to the next variation immediately.
  4. Reset: SOLID and confirmation history are scoped to the current phase and current variation.
Safety Note

Stiffness is not the same as pain

Mild stiffness that eases as you warm up is common during a return from prolonged sitting. Stop or regress an exercise if you feel sharp, radiating, worsening, or neurologic symptoms, or if pain changes your movement. This tracker is not medical care.

iPhone / iPad Installation

Add Comeback Blueprint to your Home Screen

  1. Open the hosted app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Confirm the name, then tap Add.

Launch it from the new Home Screen icon. It will open in standalone mode and remain usable offline after the first successful load.

Checking offline support…

Installation requires the files to be hosted over HTTPS. Opening index.html directly from the Files app will not register the offline service worker.

Data is stored on this device. A real account-backed service would be required for secure multi-device sync. Video links open search results so you can choose a trusted coach or rehabilitation source.
